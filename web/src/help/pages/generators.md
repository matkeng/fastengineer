The Generators section shows what the two built-in generators add to the four [project master lists](help:master-lists).

![Generators](img/generators.png)

## Power

Every load of the Load List adds the rows its load type has for each list. The card counts the loads and load types, and the rows added to each master list.

- **Load List** opens the [Load List](help:load-list): the loads to generate from.
- **Load Types** opens the [Load Types](help:load-types): the template rows.
- **Deliverables** opens the deliverables made from the master lists.

## Instrument

Every row of the Instrument Runsheet adds the rows its equipment type has for each list. The card counts the runsheet rows (and how many are not confirmed), and the rows added to each master list.

- **Instrument Runsheet** opens the [runsheet](help:instrument-runsheet).
- **Equipment Types** opens [Equipment Packages](help:equipment-packages), where each type's rows are edited.

## There is nothing to run

Both generators are always up to date. A master list is worked out whenever it is opened, from the Load List, the runsheet and the templates as they are at that moment.

## Generators from imported workbooks

A project made in an earlier version may also list generators that were imported from a workbook. They can still be opened, edited, checked, built, compared with the previous build, exported and deleted, and deliverables that use them keep working. New ones cannot be imported in the app.
