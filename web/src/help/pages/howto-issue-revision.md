## 1. Make the deliverable

1. Open **Deliverables** and press **New deliverable**.
2. Choose the **Type**, type a **Name**, and choose what the content comes from.
3. Press **Create**, then fill in the **Document number** and **Document title**. They name the exported files.

![A new deliverable](img/deliverable-new.png)

## 2. Choose its rows and columns

- **Only rows where**: choose a column and the values to keep, for example *Plant Area* is *100*. Leave it at *every row* for the whole list.
- **Choose and order…**: tick the columns you want and put them in order. Tag (or Cable Number) has to stay, because revisions are compared by it.

Press **Save**.

## 3. Review, then issue

The **Rows** tab under **Current (not issued)** shows exactly what would be issued. Read the warnings above it. When it is right:

1. Press **Issue Rev A…**.
2. Type a description, such as *Issued for review*.
3. Confirm.

![Issuing Rev A](img/deliverable-issue-dialog.png)

The rows and columns are frozen as Rev A, with the date and your name.

## 4. Export it

In the **Revisions** table, press **Excel** or **CSV** on the revision. The file is named with the document number, the revision letter and the title.

![An issued revision](img/deliverable-issued.png)

## 5. See what has changed since

As the Load List, the templates or the runsheet change, the deliverable follows. The blue panel counts the differences from the latest revision, and the **Changes** tab lists them:

- rows **added** and **removed**;
- rows **changed**, with old → new for each column.

## 6. Issue the next revision

Press **Issue Rev B…**. Rev B keeps its rows and its changes against Rev A, which you can view at any time with **View**.

## 7. Withdraw a revision issued by mistake

Only the **latest** revision can be withdrawn:

1. Press **Withdraw** on it.
2. Type the reason and confirm.

Its frozen copy is deleted and the letter is used by the next issue. The change log records the withdrawal.

## Good to know

- Issued revisions cannot be edited. Only the description can be amended.
- A deliverable with revisions cannot be deleted.
- A load with no load type blocks issuing. The message names the loads.
